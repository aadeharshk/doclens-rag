# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (Sample, Fictional Company)

This handbook is a fictional sample document used to demonstrate DocLens RAG. Northwind Labs is not a real company.

## 1. Working Hours

Core working hours at Northwind Labs are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may plan their own schedule as long as they complete 40 hours per week. Team stand-up meetings happen every weekday at 11:15 AM and should not run longer than 15 minutes.

## 2. Hybrid Work Policy

Employees in the Mumbai office work on a hybrid model. Every employee must be in the office on Tuesday and Thursday, which are designated collaboration days. The remaining three days can be worked from home. Employees who live more than 60 km from the office may apply to their manager for a fully remote arrangement, which is reviewed every six months.

## 3. Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month. Up to 8 unused leave days can be carried forward to the next year; any balance above 8 days lapses on 31 December. Employees also receive 10 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

## 4. Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption. Adoptive parents of a child below 3 months of age receive 12 weeks of paid leave.

## 5. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. During probation, the notice period is 15 days on either side. After confirmation, the notice period increases to 60 days. Probation can be extended once by up to 3 months if the manager documents specific performance gaps.

## 6. Salary and Payroll

Salaries are credited on the last working day of every month. Payslips are available in the HR portal by the 3rd working day of the following month. Any payroll error must be reported to payroll@northwindlabs.example within 10 days of the salary credit date.

## 7. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, a manager assessment and peer feedback from at least two colleagues. Annual salary revisions are announced after the April review cycle and take effect from 1 July.

## 8. Learning and Development

Every full-time employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000, which can be used for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Certification exam fees are reimbursed only after the employee passes the exam. Employees can also take up to 5 paid learning days per year for training or conferences.

## 9. Travel and Expenses

Business travel must be approved by the manager before booking. Domestic flights must be booked in economy class. The daily meal allowance during domestic business travel is INR 1,500. Expense claims, with receipts attached, must be submitted within 30 days of the trip ending; claims submitted later are not reimbursed.

## 10. Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop on their first day. Engineers receive a laptop with at least 32 GB of RAM. Employees working from home can claim a one-time home-office allowance of INR 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. Laptops must be returned to IT on the last working day.

## 11. Information Security

All laptops must use full-disk encryption and auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long, and multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email and code repositories. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwindlabs.example within 2 hours.

## 12. Use of AI Tools

Employees may use approved AI assistants for drafting, coding and analysis. Customer data, personal data and source code from client projects must never be pasted into AI tools that are not on the approved list. The approved list is maintained by the Security team and reviewed every quarter.

## 13. Health Insurance

Employees are covered by group health insurance with a sum insured of INR 5 lakh per year. The cover includes the employee's spouse, up to two children and parents or parents-in-law. Pre-existing conditions are covered from the first day. Employees can buy a top-up cover of an additional INR 10 lakh at a subsidised premium.

## 14. Referral Bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus of INR 50,000 for engineering roles and INR 25,000 for all other roles. The bonus is paid after the new hire completes 3 months with the company. Referrals of former employees who left within the past 12 months are not eligible.

## 15. Grievance Redressal

Employees can raise a grievance with their manager, HR business partner or anonymously through the ethics hotline. HR acknowledges every grievance within 2 working days and aims to resolve it within 30 days. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee under the POSH Act.

## 16. Exit Process

Employees who resign must serve their notice period unless the manager approves an early release. Unused annual leave is encashed in the full and final settlement, up to a maximum of 30 days. The full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day, and the experience letter is issued on the last working day.
